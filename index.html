Jie Su  |  苏捷🧑‍🚀

I am an Assistant Professor at the Institute of Cyber Security, Zhejiang University of Technology. My research lies at the intersection of signal processing and machine learning, with a particular focus on continual and data-efficient intelligent systems.

Prior to joining ZJUT, I was fortunate to be advised by Prof. Rajiv Ranjan and Prof. Graham Morgan at Newcastle University. I received my master's degree with distinction from the University of Southampton under the supervision of Prof. Adam Prugel-Bennett.
